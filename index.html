✦ ABOUT TEAM

Team Phoenix is a hackathon squad built on collaboration, curiosity, and the hunger to build things that matter. We don't wait for opportunities – we create them.

We are a group of developers who believe that the best way to learn is by building. Whether it's a 48-hour hackathon or a month-long project, we show up prepared, work as a unit, and push each other to be better.

The Name – Why Phoenix?
The phoenix rises from the ashes. We've failed. We've crashed. We've had code break at 4 AM. But every time, we get back up, learn what went wrong, and come back stronger.

“We don't rise to the level of our goals, we fall to the level of our systems.”
~ James Clear
